## CommandSite Services Agreement

This Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of **{{effective_date}}** ("Effective Date") between:

- **CommandSite LLC**, a {{state_of_incorporation}} limited liability company with its principal place of business at {{commandsite_address}} ("CommandSite"), and
- **{{customer_legal_name}}**, a {{customer_entity_type}} with its principal place of business at {{customer_address}} ("Customer").

CommandSite and Customer may be referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

---

### 1. Services

CommandSite will design, deploy, and operate a custom AI employee ("{{persona_name}}") for Customer, including:

- Initial setup of Customer's dedicated dashboard at `commandsite.io/dashboard/{{customer_slug}}`
- Configuration of the following AI roles enabled per Discovery Brief: **{{enabled_roles_list}}**
- Ongoing operation, monitoring, and refinement of {{persona_name}}'s outputs
- Customer support during business hours (Mon–Fri, 9am–5pm ET)

The specific scope of {{persona_name}}'s work is defined by the Discovery Brief returned by Customer on **{{discovery_brief_date}}** and may be revised in writing by mutual agreement.

### 2. Term

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of **twelve (12) months** ("Initial Term"). After the Initial Term, this Agreement automatically renews for successive twelve-month periods unless either Party provides at least **thirty (30) days' written notice** of non-renewal prior to the end of the then-current term.

### 3. Fees and Payment

**Monthly subscription fee:** ${{monthly_rate}} USD, billed on the {{billing_day}} day of each calendar month via automatic charge to the payment method on file.

**Founding partner rate (if applicable):** The monthly rate above reflects Customer's founding-partner pricing, which is locked for the Initial Term. After the Initial Term, the rate may increase to CommandSite's then-standard rate with at least sixty (60) days' written notice.

**Setup fee:** {{setup_fee}} USD, due on the Effective Date.

**Late payment:** Invoices not paid within fifteen (15) days of the due date may accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum allowed by law, whichever is less.

### 4. Customer Responsibilities

Customer agrees to:

- Provide accurate information in the Discovery Brief and respond to clarifying questions within a reasonable time
- Grant CommandSite the access necessary to operate {{persona_name}} (e.g., email account OAuth, calendar, CRM as applicable)
- Review and approve drafts during shadow mode within a reasonable cadence (typically daily for the first two weeks)
- Maintain a valid payment method on file
- Refrain from using CommandSite or {{persona_name}} for any unlawful purpose, spam, harassment, or content that violates applicable platform policies (Gmail, Twilio, etc.)

### 5. Data + Privacy

**Customer Data.** Customer retains all rights to its customer lists, conversation history, business operations data, and any other information Customer provides to CommandSite ("Customer Data"). CommandSite will use Customer Data only to provide the Services.

**CommandSite Technology.** CommandSite retains all rights to the CommandSite platform, {{persona_name}}'s underlying models and prompts, role library, and any improvements developed in the course of providing the Services that are not specific to Customer Data.

**Confidentiality.** Each Party will keep the other's non-public information confidential and use it only as needed to perform under this Agreement. This obligation survives termination of this Agreement.

**Data return.** Upon termination, Customer may request export of its data in a commercially reasonable format within thirty (30) days. After ninety (90) days post-termination, CommandSite may delete Customer Data from active systems.

### 6. Acceptable Use

Customer will not, and will not permit any third party to:

- Use {{persona_name}} to send unsolicited bulk communications (spam) in violation of CAN-SPAM, TCPA, GDPR, or other applicable laws
- Misrepresent {{persona_name}}'s identity in a way that constitutes fraud or impersonation
- Use the Services to send content that is defamatory, infringing, or unlawful

CommandSite reserves the right to suspend the Services if it has a reasonable basis to believe Customer is violating this section, with as much notice as is reasonable under the circumstances.

### 7. Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, neither Party will be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, consequential, special, or punitive damages. Each Party's aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement is capped at the total amount Customer paid CommandSite under this Agreement in the **twelve (12) months** preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

This cap does not apply to: (a) breaches of confidentiality, (b) indemnification obligations under Section 8, or (c) Customer's payment obligations.

### 8. Indemnification

Customer will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless CommandSite from third-party claims arising from Customer Data or Customer's misuse of the Services in violation of Section 6.

CommandSite will defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Customer from third-party claims that the CommandSite platform itself (as delivered, excluding Customer Data and Customer-directed configurations) infringes a U.S. patent, copyright, or trademark.

### 9. Termination

**For convenience:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon **thirty (30) days' written notice**. Fees already paid for the current billing period are non-refundable except as noted below.

**For cause:** Either Party may terminate immediately upon written notice if the other Party (a) materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure within fifteen (15) days, or (b) becomes insolvent or files for bankruptcy.

**Effect of termination:** Customer's access to the dashboard and {{persona_name}}'s active operations end on the termination effective date. Sections 5 (Data + Privacy), 7 (Limitation of Liability), 8 (Indemnification), and 11 (Governing Law) survive termination.

### 10. Service Levels

CommandSite will use commercially reasonable efforts to keep the Services available. CommandSite does not commit to a specific uptime SLA during the Initial Term given the early-stage nature of the platform; this Agreement may be amended to add a formal SLA in subsequent renewal terms.

### 11. Governing Law + Disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of **{{governing_state}}**, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles. The Parties agree that any dispute will be resolved in the state or federal courts located in {{venue_county}} County, {{governing_state}}.

### 12. Miscellaneous

- **Entire agreement:** This Agreement, including any Discovery Brief or Statement of Work attached or referenced, is the entire agreement between the Parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior agreements on the same subject.
- **Modifications:** Any modification must be in writing and signed by both Parties.
- **Assignment:** Neither Party may assign this Agreement without the other's consent, except in connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of substantially all assets.
- **Force majeure:** Neither Party will be liable for delays caused by events beyond reasonable control (natural disasters, government actions, internet outages affecting major providers, etc.).
- **Counterparts:** This Agreement may be signed electronically and in counterparts.

## How to use this template

1. **Get a lawyer to review the language** before sending to your first customer. The structure here is reasonable for a $500–$1500/mo SaaS deal but should be reviewed in your jurisdiction.
2. **Variables to fill per deal:**
   - `{{effective_date}}`, `{{customer_legal_name}}`, `{{customer_entity_type}}`, `{{customer_address}}`
   - `{{persona_name}}` ("Ada" or "Grace")
   - `{{customer_slug}}` (the dashboard URL slug)
   - `{{enabled_roles_list}}` (comma-separated list from Discovery Brief)
   - `{{discovery_brief_date}}`
   - `{{monthly_rate}}`, `{{billing_day}}`, `{{setup_fee}}`
   - `{{state_of_incorporation}}`, `{{commandsite_address}}`
   - `{{governing_state}}`, `{{venue_county}}`
   - Signature names + titles
3. **Save the rendered contract** as a PDF and upload to Dropbox Sign / PandaDoc / whatever you pick for e-signature.
4. **Paste the final URL** into the onboarding drawer's "Mark contract sent" action so it's captured in `contract_url`.

## Notes for adapting this template

- **Term length.** 12 months is the founding-partner default. For non-founding customers post-launch, consider month-to-month with 30-day notice.
- **Founding-partner clause.** Remove section 3's founding-partner paragraph for non-founding customers.
- **Setup fee.** If you're not charging one, delete the setup-fee line entirely.
- **Indemnification.** The IP indemnity in section 8 is moderate. A more aggressive customer may want broader coverage; a cautious lawyer will narrow it. Discuss with counsel.
- **Liability cap.** 12-months-of-fees is industry-standard for SMB SaaS. Some customers will push for higher caps in negotiation — be ready with a rationale.
