An honest boundary

What Grace is not.

We've been in church ministry too long to pretend AI replaces pastoral care. Here's what Grace doesn't do:

Not a replacement for your pastoral team.

Grace drafts. Your team sends. Every message that leaves your church is a message a human on your staff approved. Period.

Not an autonomous AI.

She doesn't decide when to act on her own. She runs on a predictable schedule you can set, with full transparency on every draft.

Not a chat bot for your congregation.

Your members never talk to Grace. Grace works internally for your staff. The texts and emails that go out come from your pastor's number, your church's email.

Not a way to fake care.

Grace surfaces who needs care. She doesn't pretend to care for them. The relational work is still yours, and that's the point.
