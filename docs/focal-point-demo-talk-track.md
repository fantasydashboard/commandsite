# Focal Point Church: Demo Talk Track

**The demo, July 14. Grace on Focal Point's real Planning Center data.**

---

## The frame (say this first, before you click anything)

> "Everything you're about to see is your real data, pulled from your Planning Center this week. No mockups. And it's not four dashboards, it's one assistant, Grace, watching your whole week, the front door and the back door, so nobody slips through either."

The through-line for the whole demo: **one employee, two doors.** Front Desk is the front door (guests arriving), Care & Drift is the back door (members drifting), and the two hand off to each other.

---

## Run of show

### 1. Today: the hub (open here)
- **Read Grace's Monday note out loud.** It's the "employee who briefs you before staff meeting."
- **Land on the money line:** *"Before anything else on Sunday: the Media Team is not on the schedule for the next three weeks, and it serves every single week. Nobody set it up."*
- Then: *"Everything Grace flagged links straight into the page where I act."* **Click a "Your Monday" row** to jump into a page.
- **Proves:** Today is the front door to the whole system, and it's honest, real counts, real names, no fabrication.

### 2. Front Desk & Guests: the front door
- Show the **guest pipeline** (New → Welcomed → Returned → Connecting → Belongs, plus Cooled).
- **The line:** *"29 of your 30 most recent guests are still just visitors. This board is how you close that gap instead of hoping they come back."*
- Approve a welcome draft inline to show the human gate.
- **Proves:** guests get walked from first visit to belonging, automatically.

### 3. Care & Drift: the back door
- Show the **pipeline** (Families drifting + Stopped serving), then the **priority strip** ("Needs you this week").
- **Open the Leader Digest** and **tap "I reached out" live.** Say: *"Your leaders never open a dashboard. They get one email Monday and tap one button, and it moves the card on my board."*
- Point at the cross-track card (Manuel, promoted from serving to pastoral): *"He stopped serving and now he's not attending. That's not a volunteer problem anymore, it's someone leaving, so Grace hands him to the pastor."*
- **Proves:** nobody falls through the cracks, and the right human is always the one who reaches out.

### 4. Sundays & Comms: the jaw-drop
- Lead with the **scheduling health grid.** Point at **Media Team, "not scheduled" three Sundays running.** *"A normal tool never catches this, because Planning Center has nothing to report when nobody sets a team up. Grace knows it runs every week, so she flags it."*
- Then the **burnout-aware fills:** *"41 open spots this weekend. Here's who to ask, and just as important, who not to, I'm not suggesting Cindy, she's already serving five times a month."*
- **Proves:** Sunday gets staffed weeks ahead, without burning out your faithful few.

### 5. Insights: proof it sees the church better than they do
- **Are we growing:** 862 last Sunday, up ~6.9% year over year, with Grace's read handling the Easter shift.
- **Body health:** *"About 1 in 4 of your core adults serve. And you're a strong 35-to-54 church but thin on young adults, under 4%."*
- **The database reality:** *"You have 12,000 active records in Planning Center, but only about 1,750 are actual members or regular attenders. Grace works off the real core, not the noise."*
- **Proves:** the tool understands their church better than their own spreadsheet.

## If they ask (honest answers ready)

- **"Why does it say July 5, not this past Sunday?"** → Attendance comes from your weekly summary sheet, which runs through July 5. It updates the moment you send the new numbers. (Everything from Planning Center directly, guests, roster, serving, is current.)
- **"Where's giving? Where are groups?"** → Those light up the moment Christina's full-access token is connected. Nothing's faked, they're marked pending on purpose.
- **"Does it send things without us?"** → Never. Grace drafts, a human approves. No auto-send, and no giving dollar amounts on screen, participation only.
- **"How does it know who to ask for a role?"** → Serving history plus burnout awareness. It won't ask someone already overloaded, and it surfaces people who show up every week and have never been asked.
- **"Is the 2025 comparison real?"** → That specific year-over-year line is sample data until you send last year's summary sheet. It's marked with an asterisk. Everything else is real.
