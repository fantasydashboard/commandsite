## Section 3 — What Ada Actually Does

**Section heading:** Five things Ada handles for you.

Five module cards in a 2x3 grid (or vertical stack on mobile). Each card: icon + title + 1-line + 1 sentence of detail.

### 📞 Ada at the front desk
**Catches every call. Books every job.**
Trained on your services, pricing, hours, and dispatch rules. Answers in your business's voice, books straight to your calendar, escalates emergencies to your cell — 24/7. Sounds like a thoughtful office manager, not a chatbot.

### 📋 Ada chases your quotes
**No more estimates collecting dust.**
Every quote you send gets a 7-day SMS follow-up sequence in your voice. Ada answers basic questions, schedules walk-throughs, and only pings you when a serious lead needs a human.

### ⭐ Ada asks for the review
**At the moment customers are happiest.**
Ada texts customers 2 hours after job completion — the highest-converting window. She drafts your responses to anything 3 stars or below before they go live, so a bad review never sits unanswered.

### 🔁 Ada wakes up old customers
**The leads you forgot about? She didn't.**
Ada pulls dormant leads and past customers from your CRM, segments by job type and time silent, and runs personalized re-engagement campaigns. Most owners book 4-8 jobs in the first 30 days from leads they'd written off.

### 📊 Ada's daily report
**One screen. Everything that matters.**
Calls handled, quotes sent, reviews earned, jobs booked. No bouncing between tabs. No "wait, which tool does that live in?" Open it in the morning, see what Ada handled overnight, and get back on the truck.

**Plus — Ask Ada anything.** A chat box right on your dashboard: *"Ada, did the Whitaker quote get a response?" "Ada, who haven't I followed up with this week?" "Ada, what came in overnight?"* She knows your business — ask her like she's your office manager.

## Section 7 — FAQ

**Section heading:** Common questions

Six FAQs, accordion-style. Each one short and direct.

**Q: I already use ServiceTitan / Jobber / Housecall Pro. Does this replace it?**
No. CommandSite plugs into your existing CRM, scheduling, and invoicing. We're the missing layer between your customers and your tools — the part that catches calls, chases quotes, and collects reviews automatically. Your existing stack stays.

**Q: How long does setup actually take?**
14 days from kickoff to live. The first week is us learning your business + building your custom AI. The second week is testing, tuning, and going live with monitoring. Most owners are surprised how little of their time it takes.

**Q: What if I'm too small? I only do 60 jobs a month.**
Our Starter tier is built for exactly that range — small teams who can't justify a full-time CSR but are losing real money to missed calls and forgotten quotes. If 60 jobs/month feels like the ceiling and you want more, this is for you.

**Q: What about my data? Where does it live?**
Your data lives in a dedicated, isolated environment — not shared with other customers. We use the same security standards as the major business apps you already use. We never sell or share your data, and you own everything.

**Q: What if it doesn't work for me?**
Cancel anytime after month 1. The first-month investment covers your custom build — after that, it's straight monthly. If the system isn't earning its keep, you're not locked in.

**Q: Are you a real person? This feels like a small operation.**
It is. CommandSite is built and run by Josh — a solo founder who spent a decade with service businesses before building this. That's the point. You're not buying enterprise SaaS — you're getting a system (and an AI employee) that's been thought through by someone who actually knows what your back office looks like.

**Q: Why is she called Ada?**
Named after Ada Lovelace, who wrote the first computer program in the 1840s — a hundred years before computers existed. She's the original AI ancestor. Naming our AI employee after her is our small way of honoring that lineage. Plus, "Ada" is short, easy to say on a phone, and sounds like a name you'd actually want answering for your business.

**Q: Does Ada say she's an AI when she answers calls?**
Yes — Ada always identifies herself as your AI assistant on the first interaction. We think transparency is the right move (and it's becoming legally required in some states). Once customers know she's AI, they're often impressed at how naturally she handles the call.
