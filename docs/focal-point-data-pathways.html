Grace: where every number comes from

A page by page map of what Grace reads out of Planning Center, the rule she applies to it, and how current it is. Written so you can check any number on the dashboard against the same record in Planning Center.

Prepared for Christina Spoon and Andrew Daniel · September 14, 2026 · Josh Daniel, CommandSite

How the connection works

Grace reads Planning Center through your church's own authorized connection. She never writes to Planning Center. Nothing on the dashboard moves a card, advances a step, or changes a record. Every action stays in your team's hands.

Between 4:00 and 5:00 each morning, Grace pulls seven sets of records and copies them into our database, one job per set, ten minutes apart. Then she runs the rules described below over that copy and stores the result for each page.

Refresh this page does the same thing on demand, for only what is in front of you. On Front Desk that is the Starting Point cards; on Care & Drift it is whichever tab is open. It is deliberately not a whole-church refresh, because nobody needs all seven at once and asking for them together is slower and less reliable. Settings has a Refresh everything button for the rare case you want the lot.

Three labels appear throughout this document:

  • Live updates itself every night from Planning Center.
  • Snapshot is real Planning Center data from a pull on a stated date, but it does not refresh on its own yet.
  • Your sheet comes from the weekly summary sheet or the Metrics workbook, not from Planning Center.

Two separate counts: cards created in a month, and cards completed in a month. They are drawn apart on purpose and never divided into each other, because someone who completed Starting Point in August almost certainly first visited months earlier.

The one gap on this page

Planning Center has no record of an adult returning to a service. Only Kids and Young Adults check in. So Grace cannot see a second visit, and a card only moves when your team moves it. Everything above works around that rather than pretending otherwise.

Care & Drift

Answers: who was here regularly and has quietly stopped.

Families drifting Live

Source: Kids check-ins, grouped by Planning Center household.

Rule: the family must be established first, meaning 8 or more Sundays checked in within a 10 month window. Then they are flagged once they have missed 3 or more Sundays in a row. Families still in their first weeks are held back rather than flagged, so a new family is never chased for missing a week.

Grouping by household is what stops "The Farmer family" and "The Farmer jr family" reading as two different families.

Stopped serving Live

Source: the Services schedule, not check-ins. People serve without checking in and whole teams skip the kiosk, so a missing check-in never means someone stopped.

Rule: a regular volunteer is someone with 4 or more confirmed dates. They are flagged when their last confirmed date is 6 or more weeks ago and they have nothing upcoming on the schedule. Staff are excluded by name. If every team a person served on has gone quiet, they are excluded too, because that is a retired team rather than a drifting person.

Group drift Live

Source: Growth Groups attendance.

Rule: the group needs at least 4 meetings on record and the person at least 5 attendances. They are flagged when they have missed the group's last 3 meetings and at least 3 weeks have passed. Anyone who attended one of the last three meetings is never flagged, so someone who walks back in comes off the list on their own.

The season starts September 1, 2025 and runs to today.

Known problem, being fixed this week

That season now spans two program years. It covers fall 2025, spring 2026, the summer break, and the fall that started six weeks ago. Because groups re-form each year, someone who attended faithfully through May and is not in this year's rotation currently reads as drifting.

I checked this against your live group data rather than guessing. Of the people on the list today, about a third were last seen in August or later and are genuinely drifting now. The rest were last seen in the previous program year. Splitting those two groups is the fix, the same way Front Desk separates this week's guests from people waiting on a decision. Until it ships, treat the older end of that list as last year's roster rather than this week's work.

Serving

Answers: what is short this Sunday, who can fill it, and who is carrying too much.

Needs you this Sunday, and the four week grid Live

Source: the Sunday Service plans in Services. Grace reads the last 8 plans to learn which teams normally run, then the next 4 to see what is short.

For each team on each plan she reads two things: who is scheduled, split into confirmed, unconfirmed and declined; and the positions still listed as needed. "Short" means Planning Center still shows positions needed. "Not scheduled" means the team runs most Sundays but is absent from that plan entirely, which is the case a needed positions view alone can never catch. A Sunday nobody has built yet shows as a dash rather than a wall of red.

Vocals and Band are folded into Worship, since you merged those teams and Planning Center keeps the history under the old names.

Who to ask Live

Source: Growth Group membership plus Kids check-ins, for people who serve on nothing.

Rule: ranked by the two signals that predict a yes. Tier 1, shown by name, is people in a Growth Group and dropping kids off. Tier 2 is group membership only and Tier 3 is kids drop-off only; both are reported as counts because there is no attendance measure behind them. Anyone already at high serving load is filtered out, so a name suggested here is never a name the burnout list is protecting.

You can remove anyone from this list yourself, and it stays removed for your whole team.

The link from a child's check-in back to a parent is Planning Center's own checked in by field, which is a real adult person record. Staff are excluded using the staff list in your settings.

How the load is spread, and Burnout Watch Live

Source: confirmed serving assignments over a 6 month season.

Rule: a person is flagged at 3 or more shifts a month, or on 2 or more teams at any frequency. The headline count uses only the 3 or more a month group, because "serving too often" is not true of someone on two teams once a month. High risk means 4 or more shifts a month or three or more teams.

When you pick English or Brazilian, each person's load is recalculated from only that congregation's shifts. Someone serving the 6pm service seven times and an English service twice is a real concern for one ministry and a light load for the other.

Why one person can show two numbers

The fill suggestions use a 90 day window, because "who has room this Sunday" is a recent question. Burnout Watch uses the 6 month season, because risk builds up over time. So the same person can read 10x a month in one place and 9x in another. Both are correct; each now names its own window.

The congregation switch

The English, Brazilian and All control at the top of every page scopes the whole dashboard, not one panel. People lists, serving load, groups and guests all follow it.

A person's congregation is worked out from what they do: which teams they serve on, and which groups they belong to. Where we have no record either way, the person appears under All and is reported as unplaced rather than quietly hidden. On Insights, the panels that cannot be split (multi-year growth, salvations, age, online, youth, and the roll counts) are labelled church-wide.

Known gaps, stated plainly

  • Adult attendance is not in Planning Center. Only Kids and Young Adults check in, so no page can see an adult return. This is the single biggest limit on the guest pages.
  • Half of Insights waits on your sheet. Until Grace can read the headcounts page, those panels stay at July.
  • The Today letter is not live yet. Being fixed.
  • Group drift spans two program years. Described above. Being fixed this week.
  • Two smaller pieces still update by hand: the English and Brazilian lookup behind the congregation switch, and the recent-activity panel inside a person's detail drawer. Neither changes what is counted, but both go stale for people who joined recently.
  • Giving is not connected. The panel is there and empty. It would show participation only, never dollar figures, and only if you want it.
  • Sundays counted for a guest are kids drop-offs. Someone who attends without children will undercount.
If a number looks wrong, the fastest way to tell me is the number you expected and the page you saw it on. Every figure in this document can be traced back to a specific Planning Center record, and if it cannot be, that is worth knowing too.
