Privacy Policy

Last updated July 29, 2026

CommandSite ("CommandSite," "we," "us") operates the CommandSite platform at commandsite.io, including the Grace and Ada AI assistants used by our customers (churches and local service businesses). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It includes specific disclosures about our use of Google user data.

Who this covers

This policy covers our customers (organizations that subscribe to CommandSite) and the authorized users who connect accounts and use the dashboard on their organization's behalf.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email, organization, role, and login credentials managed through our authentication provider.
  • Connected-service data: when you connect a third-party account (for example, Planning Center or Google), we access data from that service, as described below, to provide the features you enable.
  • Usage data: standard logs needed to operate and secure the service.

Google user data (Gmail)

When an authorized user connects a Google account, CommandSite requests these Google OAuth scopes:

  • gmail.send to send emails on your behalf.
  • openid and userinfo.email to identify which email address you connected.

How we use this access

  • We use gmail.send only to send emails that a member of your organization has reviewed and approved (for example, a guest welcome or a follow-up note). We send from the address you connected, so replies go to your own inbox.
  • We use the account email only to display and route which connected address a message is sent from.

What we do not do

  • We do not read, download, index, or store the contents of your Gmail mailbox. The gmail.send scope is send-only and does not grant, and we do not request, read access to your email.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, and we do not sell it.
  • We do not allow humans to read your Google data, except as necessary for security purposes (such as investigating abuse), to comply with applicable law, or where you have given us explicit consent.

Storage and security

  • Your Google OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM and are accessible only to our secured server processes. They are never exposed to the browser or to other customers.
  • You can revoke CommandSite's access at any time from your dashboard (Settings, then Integrations, then Disconnect) or from your Google Account security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all sending and deletes the stored connection.

Limited Use disclosure

CommandSite's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other connected services

When you connect other services (such as Planning Center), we access only the data needed to provide the features you turn on, and we handle it under the same security and non-sale commitments described above.

How we use information

To provide, secure, and improve the service, to send communications you or your organization have approved, and to comply with legal obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us operate the platform (such as our hosting and database providers), under contracts that protect it, or where required by law.

Data retention

We keep information for as long as your organization uses the service, and delete or anonymize it on request or when it is no longer needed, subject to legal requirements.

Your choices

You can disconnect any connected service, request access to or deletion of your information, and contact us with any privacy question.

Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the updated version here with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: josh@commandsite.io.
