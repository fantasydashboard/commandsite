The platform

CommandSite

CommandSite is an AI assistant platform for small businesses and organizations. It connects to the tools a team already uses, like Google Workspace (Gmail) and Planning Center, and helps them get recurring work done: drafting follow-up messages, surfacing who needs attention, and keeping records clean. A person on the team reviews and approves every message before it is sent.

What CommandSite does

  • Connects your tools. A team securely links the accounts they already use so CommandSite can work alongside them, not replace them.
  • Drafts and sends approved messages. CommandSite drafts follow-ups, for example a welcome to a new guest, and once a staff member approves, sends them from the organization's own connected email address.
  • Surfaces what needs attention. It highlights the people and tasks worth acting on, so nothing falls through the cracks.
  • Keeps you in control. Every outbound message is reviewed and approved by a person. Nothing is sent automatically without approval.

How CommandSite uses Google data

When you connect a Google account, CommandSite requests send-only access (the gmail.send scope) so it can send the emails you approve, from your own address, so replies land in your inbox. It does not read, store, or index the contents of your mailbox, and it never sells your data. You can revoke access at any time from your dashboard or your Google Account settings.
